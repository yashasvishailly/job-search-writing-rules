# Worked rewrites

## Cold DM to a founder

Before:

> Hi Sarah, I hope this message finds you well! I came across Northwind and I've been following your journey since the seed round. I'm really passionate about ops and I just wanted to reach out. I don't have SaaS experience, but I'm a fast learner and I'd love the chance to chat if you're open to it. Looking forward to hearing from you!

What tripped: banned opener, research theatre, "passionate", "just wanted to reach out", named a gap she didn't have to name, three hedges, banned closer, exclamation marks.

After:

> Hi Sarah, saw the ops hire go up. The last company I joined went from 9 people to 30 in fourteen months and billing broke before anything else did. I rebuilt the invoicing flow and cut the delay in half. Worth fifteen minutes?

## Cover letter opening

Before:

> I am writing to express my interest in the Chief of Staff position at Orbit. With a proven track record of driving impactful results and a passion for helping founders scale, I believe I would be a strong addition to your dynamic team. While I don't come from a consulting background, I have leveraged my skills across multiple functions.

What tripped: banned opener, "proven track record", "impactful", "passion", "I believe", "dynamic", named the consulting gap, "leveraged".

After:

> Three companies, three founders, same first problem. Nobody owned the space between departments and the founder kept getting pulled in to route things. I built the cadence that stopped it happening, twice, and the second time it held after I left. Orbit is at the headcount where that starts.

## Application form answer

Question: Why do you want to work here?

Before:

> I'm incredibly excited about Halo's mission and I feel like my values really align with the team's. I've always been passionate about fintech and I'd love to be part of such an innovative journey.

What tripped: "incredibly", "excited", "I feel like", "align", "always been passionate", "innovative", "journey", and it says nothing.

After:

> Payments companies hit an operations wall somewhere around Series B, when the finance team is still doing reconciliation by hand and nobody has time to stop and fix it. I've sat in that seat. I want the version of the job where I get to fix it before it calcifies.

## Follow-up after silence

Before:

> Hi again! Just following up on my previous message in case it got lost. No pressure at all, but I would still love to connect if you have a few minutes. Feel free to let me know either way!

What tripped: "just", "no pressure", "but", "would love", "feel free", and it adds nothing to the first message.

After:

> Hi Sarah, following up once. The billing rebuild I mentioned took six weeks and I can walk you through exactly how, or I can leave it here. Either is fine.

## Referral ask

Before:

> Hey! Hope you're doing well. I hope you don't mind me reaching out, but I saw that your company is hiring for a role that I think might be a good fit for me. Would you be able to refer me? I'd really appreciate it!

What tripped: banned opener, "hope you don't mind", "but", "I think", "might", and it makes the reader do the work of deciding.

After:

> Hey Priya, Northwind posted an ops role on Tuesday and it's the job I've been doing for three years. Here's the two-line version you can paste if you're willing to refer me: [two lines]. If you'd rather not, say so and I'll apply cold.
