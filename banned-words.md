# Banned vocabulary

None of these appear in a job search message. Not softened, not once, not "used correctly".

## AI tell words

delve, leverage, seamless, seamlessly, robust, streamline, empower, unlock, elevate, harness, foster, facilitate, utilize, spearhead, orchestrate, navigate (as a verb for anything abstract), landscape, realm, tapestry, journey, ecosystem, synergy, holistic, granular, bandwidth, actionable, impactful, transformative, game-changing, cutting-edge, best-in-class, world-class, pivotal, crucial, vital, paramount, myriad, plethora, underscore, resonate, align (as in "aligns with my values"), passionate, dynamic, meticulous, versatile, adept, proven track record, results-driven, detail-oriented, self-starter, team player, thought leader

## Hedges

if, but, just, maybe, perhaps, might, hopefully, possibly, somewhat, slightly, kind of, sort of, a bit, I think, I believe, I feel like, I was wondering, I'd love the chance, would it be possible, I hope, happy to, feel free to, no pressure, whenever convenient, at your earliest convenience

## Banned openers

I hope this email finds you well
I hope you're doing well
I hope this message finds you
I just wanted to reach out
I'm reaching out because
I came across your profile
I stumbled upon
I've been following your journey
My name is X and I am
Allow me to introduce myself
I am writing to express my interest in
As a passionate
Quick question
Hope you don't mind me reaching out

## Banned closers

Please don't hesitate to reach out
Looking forward to hearing from you
I look forward to the opportunity
Thank you for your time and consideration
I would welcome the chance to discuss further
Let me know if you have any questions
Excited about the possibility
Let's circle back
Let's touch base
Would love to pick your brain

## Banned connective phrases

Let's dive in
Here's the thing
At the end of the day
That said
It's worth noting
Needless to say
In today's fast-paced world
In an era where
More than ever
The reality is
Let's be honest
Truth be told

## Banned describers

messy, real (as an intensifier, as in "real impact"), incredible, amazing, excited, thrilled, delighted, honored, humbled

## Punctuation and format bans

Em dashes. All of them.
Semicolons in a DM.
Exclamation marks past one per message, and one is usually one too many.
Bold, italics, headers, bullet points, numbered lists inside a DM or email body.
Emoji.
Hashtags.
ALL CAPS for emphasis.
